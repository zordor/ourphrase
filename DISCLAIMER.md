# Disclaimer

OurPhrase is a supplementary identity-checking aid, not proof of identity,
an emergency service, authentication for regulated transactions, or a
replacement for professional security controls.

Matching words indicate that two devices possess the same shared key at that
time. They do not prove who is physically holding either device. A compromised
or unlocked device, exposed invitation, coerced user, incorrect device clock,
screen sharing, shoulder surfing or maliciously modified build can undermine
that signal.

Do not rely on OurPhrase alone for payments, account recovery, medical or legal
decisions, physical safety, or emergencies. If anything feels wrong, stop and
verify through another trusted channel. The software is provided “as is”,
without warranty, as stated in the MPL-2.0 license.
